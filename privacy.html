Privacy Policy

AlessandroDigital Uploader

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Who we are

AlessandroDigital Uploader is operated by Alessandro Rosa, Germany, owner of the Etsy shop AlessandroDigital.

2. What the app does

The app only accesses the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official API in order to upload the owner's own short videos showcasing his digital templates.

3. Data we collect

The app collects no personal data from other users or visitors. It has no third-party users.

4. Data we store

The only data stored are the access tokens required to connect to the owner's TikTok account. These tokens are stored securely by the integration provider Composio and are used only to perform uploads on the owner's behalf.

5. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

6. Your rights

Since no personal data of other people is processed, there is no third-party data to access or delete. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok's settings.

7. Contact

For any privacy question, please contact us via Etsy messages in the shop alessandrodigital.etsy.com.
